# Working with Funders

**Status:** Drafting in progress. Section pending plain-language buildout.

This is one of the five operator-spine sections of the Commons FinOps handbook. See [`00-overview.md`](./00-overview.md) for the shape of the spine and how each section is structured.

## What this section will cover

When complete, this section will follow the standard handbook shape:

1. The compliance ground, in two paragraphs of plain language. What rule, what it requires, what happens if you miss it.
2. The operational practice, in checklist form.
3. Automation notes, called out separately.
4. What to publish, mapping each step to a field in the [funding-block v0.1 schema](../schema/SCHEMA.md).
5. Common failure modes, drawn from real cases.
6. Templates and references.

This section is anchored in **IRS Section 4945 (expenditure responsibility) and NACUBO indirect cost recovery practice**.

## Where the methodology lives today

Until this section is fully built out, the source-of-record for methodology is [`finops-handbook.md`](./finops-handbook.md), the legacy long-form reference document. The material that will become this section currently lives in **Parts 5, 7, 10, 11 of the legacy handbook** of that document. Nothing in the legacy handbook is wrong. It just is not yet in the operator-facing shape the new spine is moving toward.
