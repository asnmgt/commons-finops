# Case Studies

Operational practice in the field. These case studies describe specific decisions, the principles behind them, and the outcomes. They are the proof that Commons FinOps is a real discipline, not a vocabulary exercise.

## Index

- [`metagov-year-one.md`](./metagov-year-one.md) — Year one record of Commons FinOps practice at Metagov Inc., a 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor for digital governance research. Covers fiscal sponsorship operations, the Atlas Computing to Renaissance Philanthropy transition, the AWS gift reconciliation, financial systems and reconciliation discipline, treasury and digital assets, grant management, the endowment proposal, board and governance support, operating cadence, compliance, and year one accomplishments.

## Contributing a case study

See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md). The Metagov case study is a template for the shape: factual record of work performed, decisions reached, and principles those decisions established.
